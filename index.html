Welcome to Wood Type Wordle

Guess as many words as you like. Pick a size to start.

How to play

Guess the hidden word. Each guess is one impression on the press.

  • Pick 3, 5 or 7 letters at the top. You get one more try than the word has letters: 4, 6 or 8.
  • Every guess must be a real word of that length.
  • After you press Enter, the blocks are inked to show how close you were.
  • Stuck? Hint shows one letter in its right spot. You get 1 hint for 3 letters, 2 for 5 and 3 for 7.

Examples

PRINT

P is in the word and in the right spot.

INKED

K is in the word but in the wrong spot.

BLOCK

O is not in the word at all. Its block stays bare wood.

Play as many words as you like. When you finish one, tap Next word or press Enter for another. Each length keeps its own statistics.
